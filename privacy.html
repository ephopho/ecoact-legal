Privacy Policy

The short version: we collect nothing.

EcoAct collects no personal data. None.

No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party trackers. The app makes no network requests — it works fully offline.

· What stays on your device

Everything you create in EcoAct — your logged carbon activities, completed challenges, points, level, and streak — is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded or shared with us or anyone else. Delete the app and it's gone.

· Impact estimates

The CO₂ figures shown are approximate estimates from published averages, provided to guide and motivate — not precise measurements. They're calculated entirely on your device.

· Children

EcoAct is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
